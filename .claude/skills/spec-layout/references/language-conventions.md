## Language & rendering conventions

Part of the `spec-layout` skill — read when writing any artifact (language and rendering).

- **Kit internals in English.** Skills, **hook logic/code/comments**, gate logic, and kit docs (README, MIGRATION, DESIGN-HISTORY) are English — read by Claude (adherence is language-independent) and the kit maintainer, and aligned with the upstream sources. **Carve-out: a hook's *user-facing messages* (the block/error/rejection prose printed to the human at the moment of friction) follow the team's language (pt-BR at Sellers)** — these are read by the team, not by the upstream; the surrounding logic, comments, and **protocol tokens stay English/neutral** (see `spec-layout` → "Protocol tokens" — e.g. `Status: Approved`, `Verdict: PASS` inside a message are never translated, as the hooks/gates grep them).
- **Human-facing artifacts in the team's language.** `spec.md`, `design.md`, `tasks.md`, `evidence.md`, `SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md`, and the epic are written in the team's language (**pt-BR at Sellers**) — the team reads and *approves* them; approval in a language they don't speak is theater.
- **`encaixe` vs `seam` — two senses, one word for each.** **`encaixe`** (glossed `encaixe (seam)` on the first mention in an entry-point doc) is the *Superpowers integration point* — where the kit overrides the SP skills' defaults. **`seam`** is reserved for Michael Feathers' sense: the place where behaviour can be altered without editing in that place — the public boundary a test observes behaviour through. The English form of the first sense is a **gloss, not a free synonym**: `encaixe` is not translated, not even in English prose (same convention as **`via rápida`**, used untranslated in English prose here and in `CLAUDE.md`). Machine contracts carrying the old word are **grandfathered — never renamed**: the `<!-- sdd-kit:seam:start -->`/`:end` sentinels, the file `claudemd-seam.md`, and the identifiers in `adopt.py`/`tests/`.
- **Markdown is the single source of truth; HTML is a generated view.** Humans read/approve better in HTML, but `.md` stays canonical (diffable, gate-readable, the `Status:` of record). Generate a styled HTML *view* on demand for reading/approval — never hand-edited, never committed as a second source — so there is no sync toll. Zero-infra option for a gate review: render the artifact as a claude.ai Artifact. Team-wide option: a repo `render` script + shared CSS.
